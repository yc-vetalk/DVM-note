## p.24

• Sciatic nerve injury 坐骨神經損傷
•
The sciatic nerve is composed of a collection of 
nerves arising from the spinal cord segments of 
L6-S2.
•
The extensors of the stifle (innervated by the 
femoral nerve) are the only muscles that remain 
functional.
•
The animal may bear weight on the limb, but they 
can’t flex the stifle nor can they flex or extend the 
tarsus and digits.
•
Thus, the animal has knuckling of the digits and a 
plantigrade stance.
•
Severe muscle atrophy
•
If the nerve is impinged, severe pain results.
• Diagnosis
• Clinical exam, EMG
• Treatment options
• Either Sx or conservative Tx
• Acupuncture, Laser, and rehabilitation
• Rehabilitation plan
•
Wear booties, orthotics, Massage, PROM, CP and 
pattern exercises, UWTM
•
