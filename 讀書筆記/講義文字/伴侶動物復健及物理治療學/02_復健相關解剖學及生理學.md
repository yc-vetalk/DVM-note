## p.12

• Elastic Fibers and elastin 
彈性纖維及彈性蛋白
• Elastic fibers are yellow color and found adjacent to the skin, 
the blood vessels and in loose connective tissue. Small
percentages are also found in tendons and ligaments.
• 彈性纖維呈現黃色分佈於皮膚、血管、疏鬆結締組織，少
部分於肌腱及韌帶組織
• Elastic fibers are highly branched and form a meshwork-like 
structure. They are composed of elastin and are surrounded 
by microfibrils around elastin.
• 彈性纖維為網狀結構由微纖維圍繞彈性蛋白
• The fibers are elastic and can be extended by 70% maximally 
up to 220%.
• 延伸力可由70% 高達220%
• When tissue is subjected to force, the elastic fibers initially 
assume the load, then slowly transfer it to collagen fibers
• 組織承受力量時先由彈性纖維承受負載，然後將負載轉移
給膠原纖維

## p.19

Healing Timeline 癒合時間軸: Bone
• Inflammation
• Primary and secondary bone healing follow similar timelines. 
• Immediate to 72 hours (hematoma and inflammation, secondary bone healing)
• 初級骨癒合與次級骨癒合有相似炎症期約72小時
• Repair 
• Radiographic healing (secondary): appearance of callus at 10-12 days, disappearance of fracture line 
within 30 days.
• Disappearance of fracture line radiographically as early as 30 days with secondary bone healing. 
Primary bone healing takes longer.
• 骨痂屬於次級骨癒合出現在10-12天，影像學骨折線30天消失，初級骨癒合較次級
骨癒合慢消失
• Remodeling/Maturation 
• Woven bone development over mineralized callus. Complete remodeling of the callus 90 days after 
fracture repair for a simple fracture.
• Primary bone healing (direct union, no callus formation) produces less early stability than secondary, 
and requires implants to be in place for a longer time than with secondary healing.
• 骨痂形成編織骨到完全重建需90天
• 初級骨癒合無法提供早期穩定度，必須靠植入物提供穩定，時間比次級骨癒合更
久

## p.20

Implications for rehabilitation of bone骨骼復健的意涵
• During inflammatory phase (immediately up to 2-3 days after injury), 
decrease pain, minimize soft tissue inflammation, maintain joint 
health/Range of motion (ROM) and establish safe, controlled mobility, 
depending on fracture stability
• 在炎症期(3 天內)減少疼痛、減少發炎，骨折穩定前提下維持關節活動
• During repair phase (3 days to 4-6 weeks after injury), small, gradually 
increasing cyclic loading to increase micromotion and improve callus 
formation with secondary healing; continued maintenance of joint health 
through ROM, active or passive
• 在修補期(3 天to 4-6 週)逐漸增加骨負重增進骨痂形成進行次級骨癒合，
持續關節活動維持關節健康
