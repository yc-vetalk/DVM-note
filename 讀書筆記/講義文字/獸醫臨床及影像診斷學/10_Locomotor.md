LOCOMOTOR SYSTEM
•The abaxial part includes the anatomical structures of the 
extremities: bones (including diaphyses, epiphyses and 
apophyses, growth plates, metaphyses, sesamoid bones), joints 
(including menisci), tendons, ligaments, and muscles. This part of 
the locomotor system provides support and protection, and 
offers the possibility of standing and moving around. 
•The axial part includes the vertebral bodies, vertebral joints, 
intervertebral discs, ligaments, tendons, and muscles. The 
vertebral column protects the spinal cord and forms a connection 
with the caudal extremities via the iliosacral joints.

recurrence of problems)

CHARACTERISTIC ABNORMALITIES OF 
THE STANCE
• Varus: an angular deviation of part of 
the leg toward the median line, in the 
sagittal plane 
• Valgus: an angular deviation of part of 
the leg away from the median line, in 
the sagittal plane 
• The abnormalities are named according 
to the origin of the deviation: in animals 
whose hind legs form an X there is varus 
from the hips and valgus from the 
knees, and in those whose hind legs 
form an O there is valgus from the hips 
and varus from the knees.

• There are a few characteristic abnormalities in the positioning of the 
limb relative to the body which affect the way in which the animal 
stands: 
• Rotation: turning of a limb in a joint on its long axis 
• exorotation: lateral turning of a limb or part of a limb 
• endorotation: medial turning of a limb or part of a limb

• Adduction: entire limb moved sideways toward the median line

• Hyperextension: overstretching of joint(s) 
• Hyperflexion: overflexion (bending) of joint(s)

退化性關節疾病（degenerative joint disease :DJD） 
 骨關節病(Osteoarthritis: OA)
• 因為軟骨緩衝的機能降低，外力在軟骨下骨造成
直接的壓力

• 影響平滑的關節運動(ROM下降)

## p.148

OSTEOMYELITIS
• 早期：骨結構變得hazy (模糊、朦朧)，trabecular 外形變粗糙 
• 中期：骨吸收 同時有限度的新骨生成，如骨膜被頂起，也會有一些膜下造骨 
• 在異物如骨板或死骨(sequestrum)周圍會形成骨吸收區，這與牙根感染的周圍
骨反應相似。 
• Involucrum：在死骨片周圍區域較黑的影像 
• Sclerotic margin：病灶處可能會發展來限制感染，常見於慢性病程。 
•惡性腫瘤不容易和骨髓炎區分。 
• Loss of the normal trabecular pattern: 
• Lysis or destruction 
• Periosteal reaction：感染後7-14天漸漸明顯，也會有新骨生成、Codman’s 
triangle生成。 
• Cortical destruction：感染的地方皮質變薄，漸漸被侵蝕。 
• 與骨腫瘤的不同處是： 
• 骨新生較不嚴重(因其基本上是destructive process) 
• 發生在任何地方 
• 會跨過關節 
• 正常與不正常的骨中間界限較明顯，骨髓炎時常形成sclerotic barrier
