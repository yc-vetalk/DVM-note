Diagnostic medical procedure

How it works?
Transducer (medical imaging):3-12 MHz

the transducer

different acoustic impedance producing 
variable echogenicity

localized based on the time 
interval between emitted 
pulse and its reception.

B: higher frequency,small patient, abdomen
C: superficial structures on wide/flat surface

7
Gain setting
Ultrasound attenuate when travel through tissue
Amplification of echoes received must be adjusted 
A: excessive near gain and insufficient far gain
B: well-adjusted near and far gains
C: insufficient near gain and excessive far gain
8
Doppler effect
B: RBC moves toward this pulse, increasing the 
frequency of the returning echo. (RED) 
C: RBC moves away from this pulse, the 
frequency of the returning echo is reduced. (BLUE)
9

Examines blood flow at a specific site
velocity, direction and uniformity can be 
precisely assessed over time
