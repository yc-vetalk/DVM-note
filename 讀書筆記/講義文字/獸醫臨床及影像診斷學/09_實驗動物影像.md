X-Ray Imaging
⚫To use x-rays relying on the differential 
absorption of x-rays by tissues of various 
density & chemical composition
⚫more x-ray are absorbed by higher density 
tissues (bone) and fewer by lower density 
tissues (lung)
⚫including x-ray computed tomograph

## p.9

Ultrasonography
⚫Use the echo of high-frequency sound to create 
visual images of internal structures
– Discriminate cystic vs. solid masses
– Interrogate body cavities filled w/ fluids (prohibit 
radiographic techniques)
– Discriminate the texture of suspected solid masses
– Locate nonradiopaque calculi w/i organs
– Biopsy guidance for internal masses & cystocentesis
– Evaluate cardiac (echocardiograph) [myocardial 
infarction in mouse], muscle, tendon
– Diagnose pregnancy (ferret, NHP)

## p.12

MRI (Magnetic Resonance Imaging)
⚫Previously called nuclear magnetic 
resonance (NMR) imaging
⚫In a magnetic field, tissues can be excited 
when irradiated w/ radiowaves at the 
resonant frequency determined by the 
magnetic field strength, then the excited 
tissue emits a radio signal that can be 
detected by a coil placed about the tissue, 
then be used to create an image.

combined w/ computed tomographic (rotating

2.
PET: utilize radionuclides that decay by positron 
emission (two photons w/ strict angular 
correlation; 180° apart)

Bioluminescence Imaging
⚫Need a gene encoding an enzyme to catalyze 
a bioluminescent reaction in animal
⚫very weak signal (but no background in mice)
⚫Two systems: firefly luciferase (630 nm, 37C)
and renilla luciferase (482 nm)
⚫Applied in
– transfected cells for tumor study
– into infectious agents (virus/bact.) for infectious D study
– gene expression study

Facility Design- general facility considerations
⚫Traffic patterns (circulation)
– minimized cross-circulation between animals and personnels
– health status of lab animals (subclinical infections)
⚫Supply storage rooms (usu. under-estimated!)
⚫Administrative & Personnel offices
⚫Break rooms
⚫Restrooms and showers (biosecurity level of animal facility)
⚫Conference room
⚫Environmental considerations (animal/personnel area w/ 
higher humidity and running equipment will generate 
lots of heat

## p.34

Animal Housing & Imaging Support
⚫Ideal: imaging center incorporates animal housing space 
or adjacent to animal holding facility
⚫Animal Housing: size & type of housing room (depend 
on species)
– rodents, NHP, …
– space for temporary quarantine of animals (before/after imaging)
⚫Procedure Rooms: usu. 1:4 ratio of procedure rooms vs
animal holding rooms
⚫Cagewash: “dirty” vs “clean” sides
⚫Husbandry supply storage
⚫Euthanasia

## p.35

Magnet-Specific Facility Design
⚫Location
– magnetic forces can inactivate/alter life-supportive devices 
(pacemaker, insulin pump)! (health hazard)
– magnetic forces may interfere other equipment
⚫Structural considerations
– MRI (radiofrequency RF to generate images) might be 
interfered by the RF of other devices. So, in RF-shielded 
rooms!
⚫Cryogen gases: need liquid Helium and liquid Nitrogen
⚫Environmental Consideration: stable temp; continual 
monitoring temp, humidity, and oxygen levels
⚫Work area and tools
