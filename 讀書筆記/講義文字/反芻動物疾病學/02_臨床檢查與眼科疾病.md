## p.2

The special stomach in ruminants
Ruminants (like cattle, sheep, goats, and deer) have one 
large stomach divided into four highly specialized 
compartments: the rumen, the reticulum, the omasum 
(foregut, 前胃), and the abomasum. This complex digestive 
setup allows them to ferment and extract nutrients from 
tough, fibrous plant materials (like grass and hay) that simple-
stomached animals cannot break down.

## p.46

Ocular Neoplasia
Although there are many types of primary and secondary tumors 
affecting the eyes and surroundings, tissue deformity and functional loss
are the most common complaints. Treatment strategies for ocular tumors 
in livestock can be various, from curative or alleviate discomfort 
(enucleation) to eradicating the problem (culling).
The classification of tumors, “benign vs. malignant" or "limited vs. 
systemic," can be used as one of the criteria for selecting a treatment for 
ocular neoplasia.
