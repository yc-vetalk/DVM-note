## p.26

Treating and Management in GI infection
Maintaining hydration and energy balance
• Moderate to severe cases: withhold milk for 24 hours
• Proving electrolytes and energy: LR + glucose 50 g, oral, 
twice (at least)
• Enrofloxacin (2.5 mg/kg, IM, sid, for 3 days)
• IV infusion: LR (1,000 mL), glucose 50 g, methionine 20 
mL
