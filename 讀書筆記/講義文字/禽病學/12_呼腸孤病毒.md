a case of synovitis(滑膜炎) in chickens

## p.7

• ubiquitous among domestic poultry 
• replicates in the gut of avian species 
• pathogenic strains can affect tendon 
• in broilers and turkeys: 
arthritis/tenosynovitis (關節炎/腱鞘炎) 
• Swelling of the hock joints (足關節) 
• lesions in the gastrocnemius tendons(腓腸腱)

## p.8

Viral arthritis 病毒性關節炎
• 早期稱tenosynovitis (腱鞘炎)
• 由ARV感染造成，常與Mycoplasma synoviae
or Staphylococcus spp. 混合感染
• co-infection: 球蟲、IBD
• mainly affects meat‐type chickens

## p.9

Host
• chickens, turkeys
• age‐associated resistance 
• 1日齡最敏感
• 自然感染多4~7週齡雞 →經濟損失
• 10週齡以上不敏感
• is commonly found in the digestive and respiratory 
tracts of clinically normal chickens and turkeys 
→difficult to interpret the relationship between 
virus isolation and disease

## p.10

Transmission
• horizontal and vertical routes
• horizontal: 
糞便→口/呼吸道→ 消化道
• Vertical: 介蛋 (非常少)

## p.11

Incubation period
• depending upon the virus pathotype, age of host, 
and route of exposure 
• 2 wks chickens→
1 day: footpad inoculation 
9 day: intratracheal inoculation 
11 days: intramuscular, intravenous inoculation
13 days: contact exposure

## p.12

Clinical Signs
• 行動不便、lameness (跛行)
• usually appears at 4–5 weeks of age although the 
infection may have occurred soon after hatching
• stunted (發育不良)、生長慢
• 羽毛生長不良
• 產蛋下降10%
• 死亡率不高 (5%)
• 發生率最高可達100%

## p.13

• 經濟損失:
• 非由死亡率導致
• diminished weight gain 體重減輕
• poor feed conversion 飼料換肉率差
• reduced marketability 適銷性降低

## p.41

Vaccination
• 有活毒跟死毒疫苗，兩者並用效果較好
• 目前對疫苗接種計畫尚無共識
• 母雞開產前2-3週注射疫苗
• Live vaccines should be administered only prior to 
the onset of egg production in order to prevent 
trans‐ovarian transmission of the vaccine virus
• 以移行抗體保護小雞 
(可保護小雞在孵出2-3週不被感染)
