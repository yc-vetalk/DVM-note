Poultry vs. Mammal
• Feathers instead of fur
• No teeth
• Lay eggs
• Float and fly
• Excrete waste through one orifice only

## p.7

Poultry vs. Mammal
• Unlike mammals, birds lack a diaphragm to inflate 
and deflate the lungs
• Birds have air sacs located in their neck and body 
cavity that inflate their lungs
• Gas exchange occurs in the lungs and the air sacs 
function to move air in and out of the respiratory system
