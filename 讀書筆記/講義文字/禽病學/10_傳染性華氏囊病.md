## p.25

Pathogenesis
Infection is the cause of immunosuppression within the first 3 weeks
Within 3-6 weeks, the clinical form of the disease occurs:
一般3-20週齡間發病，多發生在3-6週齡的雞
Clinical findings 2-3 days after infection 潛伏期2-3天
Mortality 5-8 days after infection
IBDV infects cells of the cloacal bursa – primarily immature B-cells.
mature B-cells = antibody producing cells
If B-cells are destroyed early in life the bird may become 
immunosuppressed for life.
